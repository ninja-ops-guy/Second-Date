01 THE IDEA

There’s the printed date.
Then there’s the real-life date.

Once you open something, a new little clock begins. Second Date makes that clock easy to see.

01 / OPEN IT◒

Open something good.

Pesto, oat milk, face cream, vitamins—anything with an after-opening window worth remembering.

02 / DATE IT◷

Give it a second date.

Add the opening date and the period on its label. Your personal use-by view appears in seconds.

03 / ENJOY IT♡

Use it, don’t lose it.

See what deserves attention next, finish it with satisfaction, and keep a record of what you used.
