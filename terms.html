Second Date is a personal organization tool for recording when products were opened and the after-opening periods users choose to track.

No safety determination

Second Date does not inspect products and does not determine freshness, quality, medical suitability, or safety. Dates shown by the service are reminders calculated from information entered by the user. Always follow manufacturer instructions, storage requirements, recalls, professional guidance, and applicable safety advice.

Subscriptions

If paid plans are enabled, price and billing interval are shown before checkout. Subscription management and cancellation are provided through the billing portal.

Service availability

The service may change as the product develops. Preview and beta features may be modified or withdrawn.
